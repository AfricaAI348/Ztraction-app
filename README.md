# 🚀 Ztraction

**Your Product. Seen. Guaranteed.**

Ztraction is a one-click publishing platform for vibe coders. It handles the entire app submission process so you can focus on building.

---

## What It Does

- **Publishes to Apple, Google Play, and Huawei AppGallery** in one click.
- **Auto-submits to Product Hunt, Hacker News, and 10+ launch platforms.**
- **Handles the technical nightmare** — certificates, keystores, signing, screenshots, and store rejections.
- **No Mac required. No manual uploads. No rejection loops.**

---

## Why Ztraction Exists

You can build an app in a weekend. But publishing it? That's two weeks of certificates, content ratings, and store policies before anyone can even see it.

Ztraction fixes that.
